SUP: stand up paddleboard

  • Board, skeg, ankle leash
  • Paddle
  • Chair, if that's your style
  • Pump and nozzle
  • Swimming shorts and t shirt or rash vest if it’s warm enough
  • Wetsuits and wet shoes if it's not
  • Buoyancy aid/lifejacket, with an emergency whistle attached
  • Hat/cap with retainer cord
  • Sunglasses with retainer cord

  • Evidence of right to access

  • Water bottle with clip
  • Snacks
  • Waterproof case for phone, car key and cash/payment card
  • Map and compass
  • Consider a small first aid kit
  • Consider a small packable windproof jacket

  • Sunscreen
  • Towel - ideally a surf changing towels
  • Patch kit
  • Have a dry bag for your car, to pack wet kit

    Before you go

  • Study a map of the area you're going to
  • Have a clear plan that everyone agrees
  • Inform someone not coming what the plan is and when you'll be due back
